                                         Logo
The Beaty Biodiversity Museum logo was designed
in 2005, and reflects a recurring branching pattern
found in nature within a circular field of view.



Primary
The primary logo with wordmark should be used
wherever possible. In print, the logo is rich black
CMYK 60/60/60/100.




Secondary
The secondary “stacked” logo may be used where
a vertical or more square format is needed.




Symbol
The circle without the wordmark is used rarely,
and only on occasions where the wordmark
would be redundant or illegible.




Reverse and brand colour
The logo may be reversed in white CMYK
0/0/0/0 with 100% opacity against a high-
contrast background. When a brand colour is
needed, “Beaty red” CMYK 0/100/100/0 is used.




Spacing
There should be adequate space surrounding all
sides of the logo, equivalent to half the height or
width of the circle.

                                        Fonts and text
                       Several fonts are employed by the museum. Text
                       boxes are generally set left-justified, rag-right,
                       without hyphenation at the end of lines. The museum
                       follows standard taxonomic formatting, and common
                       names of organisms are not capitalized.
